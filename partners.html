Partners and funders

Who made the study possible

AAirDS was a cross disciplinary collaboration between the NHS, universities, a public health agency and industry.

UK Health Security Agency

Funder of AAirDS; statistical and modelling collaboration.

Cambridge University Hospitals NHS Foundation Trust

Sponsor and host of the study on four medicine for older people wards at Addenbrooke's Hospital; owner of the CFD output data.

Dassault Systèmes

Industrial modelling partner: built and ran the computational fluid dynamics model of the study ward under a service agreement with the Trust.

MRC Biostatistics Unit, University of Cambridge

Trial design and statistical analysis.

University of Cambridge

Department of Medicine, Division of Anaesthesia and the Cambridge Institute of Therapeutic Immunology and Infectious Disease; on-site viral sequencing with the Department of Pathology.

MRC University of Glasgow Centre for Virus Research

Genomic epidemiology and transmission modelling.

NHS Greater Glasgow and Clyde

Clinical microbiology and infection prevention expertise.

Leeds Beckett University

Engineering, airflow and applied physiology.

University of Exeter

Geriatric medicine and epidemiology.

Air Purity, Cambridge

Supplier of the AeroTitan 2000 and 3000 portable air cleaning units used in the study.

Listed from the affiliations and acknowledgements in the AAirDS publications. Logos will be added with each organisation's agreement.
